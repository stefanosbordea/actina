What the scores mean

Precision
Of the positive predictions, how many were right?
Recall
Of the actual positive hours, how many were found?
F1
A combined measure of precision and recall.

A positive hour has radiation strictly above 600 W/m² in the Open-Meteo historical dataset. These scores do not measure curtailed electricity, water delivery or savings.

Evaluation limits

Weather comes from Open-Meteo’s historical archive, which includes model-derived data. Every retained prediction is included, including partial boundary days. Validation informed model selection. The test was already inspected before these experiments, so this is a retrospective comparison; a fresh holdout is needed for a new generalization claim.

Experiments use a 24-hour training gap and validation-selected cutoffs. They remain separate from Stefanos’s original model and scheduler.
