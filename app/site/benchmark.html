Method and downloads

What is measured

A positive hour has actual radiation strictly above 600 W/m². Precision measures how often a positive prediction is right; recall measures how many positive hours are found. F1 combines the two. This is not a measurement of grid curtailment.

Every row in Stefanos’s original prediction files is included, including incomplete boundary days. Forecast origin and target are 24 hours apart.

Limits

Original validation was used for early stopping. The experiments use a 24-hour gap before each evaluation period and choose decision cutoffs on validation only. This test was already inspected before the experiments: results are retrospective, and a fresh holdout is needed.

The original model and scheduler are preserved. The experiments produce sunny-hour classifications, not a replacement plant schedule.
