What this shows

Forecast and scheduler by Stefanos Bordea. Forecast: a LightGBM model trained on two years of Paphos weather from Open-Meteo, predicting the day ahead. Plan: the supplied Aktina scheduler output. These are historical results, not a connection to a working plant.

Plant, demand, storage and electricity prices are illustrative assumptions. A radiation threshold labels the lower-price hours. It does not measure curtailed electricity.

Cost and storage assumptions

Hourly cost = production × 3.4 ÷ 1,000 × electricity price. Price is determined by actual radiation. Daily costs compare the supplied plans. Production and ending water can differ. Tank levels are supplied end-of-hour values.
