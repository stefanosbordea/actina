Method and limits

Forecasts and event calls

Current and original forecasts use distinct retained radiation curves. Higher sensitivity keeps the original curve and changes only high-solar calls. The research comparison supplies event calls only, with the current curve shown for reference.

Mean absolute radiation error is 18.700675 W/m² for the current forecast and 18.697343 W/m² for the original. Improved event detection does not mean a more accurate radiation curve.

Historical validation

The current forecast has four fewer false alarms and one additional missed hour than the research comparison. Its F1 difference is +0.4269 percentage points, with a 95% paired-day interval from −1.6061 to +2.6215.

These hours have been inspected repeatedly. The interval includes zero, so the observed gain does not establish superiority on new data. Past observations enter only after their target time has passed.

Weather and timing

The two weather forecasts are distinct historical inputs. Day 1 and day 2 are provider labels. Their availability at the original forecast time remains unverified.

“Actual” is historical weather radiation, not a measurement of curtailed power. Times use fixed UTC+03:00. Radiation is the preceding-hour mean at each label.

Coverage

This is a historical prototype, not a live forecast or plant-control system. These comparisons do not change the schedule or its costs, and do not establish measured water savings.
