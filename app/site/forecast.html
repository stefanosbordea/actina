Sources and limits

Stefanos v2

The line is the retained predicted column from cv_predictions_v2.csv. The model adds next-day weather radiation and cloud cover to its features. The cloud readout comes from nwp_cloud in the same source snapshot’s featuresv2.csv, joined by validation origin. The radiation feature matches the supplied weather column exactly.

No new model was trained for this view. No v2 test predictions were supplied in this snapshot. Threshold 562 changes event calls only, not the radiation curve.

View source snapshot

Two weather sources

Supplied weather, day 1 is the v2 CSV’s forecast column. Its downloader requests previous_day1 from Open-Meteo without pinning a weather model.

ECMWF, day 2 is a separate retained archive pinned to ecmwf_ifs025, using shortwave_radiation_previous_day2. It is not the weather column supplied with v2.

Day 1 and day 2 are nominal provider offsets. Historical publication timing and original forecast availability remain unverified.

Reference and event model

“Actual” is the retained historical weather radiation reference. A radiation threshold is not a measurement of curtailed solar power or a water-saving result.

The two-source event comparator is f1-008/consensus_two_source, with its retained 0.50/0.60 correction rule. It supplies yes/no event calls, not a radiation forecast. Its rule was selected on validation.

Time and coverage

CSV feature timestamps are shifted forward 24 hours to their target. The recovered source clock is fixed UTC+03:00, without a seasonal clock change. Radiation is the preceding-hour mean at each target label.

The schedule page retains its original supplied plan and costs. These v2 predictions do not replace that plan’s input.
