## Sources, preflight and retained evidence

The adopted CVaR formulation is Rockafellar and Uryasev, [Conditional Value-at-Risk for General Loss Distributions](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~rtr/papers/rtr187-CVaR2.pdf), Proposition8 Eq25, Theorem10 Eqs27–30 and finite-scenario epigraph Eqs49–51. Whole-path dependence follows the distinction in [Pinson et al., From probabilistic forecasts to statistical scenarios of short-term wind power production](https://pierrepinson.com/docs/pinsonetal_wpfscenarios_fin.pdf), pp4–6 Eqs3–12 and p10. The implementation does not claim their full calibrated scenario method. [Hu and Konstantinou2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.07295), pp4–5 Eqs29–42 and pp6–7 Algorithm1, motivates explicit water/power coordination while modeling operational physics excluded here. These sources establish prior art, not this experiment's success or novelty.
