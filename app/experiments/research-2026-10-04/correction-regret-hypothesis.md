# Correction regret: proposed Aktina experiment 010

Design only, 2026-10-04; updated to the parent-approved common-issuance design before 010 freeze. No fit or new test-instance inspection performed for this proposal. The already-observed 007 failure motivates it: more recovered events also created more false alarms. The research question is whether preserving the two reference definitions and exchanging event calls can correct timing without increasing the number of positive calls.

## What is established

Full PDFs, including methods/equations, were read:

- [Mozannar and Sontag (2020)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/mozannar20b/mozannar20b.pdf), §3 Eqs.1–2 and §4 Proposition2, PDF pp.2–4: choosing a predictor versus an expert by conditional expected loss is established learning-to-defer. An automated NWP baseline can play the fallback role; this does not create a new learning principle.
- [Laroche et al. (2019)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/laroche19a/laroche19a.pdf), §2.2 Eq.5 and §2.3 Eq.8/Theorem2, pp.3–4: baseline-constrained improvement is established. Their safety result uses an MDP uncertainty set and sample-count assumptions. Two fitted irradiance-reference scores do not satisfy those assumptions, so that guarantee does not transfer.
- [Narasimhan (2018)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v84/narasimhan18a/narasimhan18a.pdf), §§1–3 and Tables1–2, pp.1–4: precision, positive-call budgets and nonlinear confusion-matrix objectives are established constrained classification problems. Optimizing our count budgets is an application of that family, not a new theorem.
- [Cotter et al. (2019)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/cotter19b/cotter19b.pdf), §§3–4 and §5, pp.2–8: meeting fitted constraints differs from generalizing them. Their practical experiment variants are distinguished from their proven algorithms. Our chronologically selected margins on a repeatedly inspected historical benchmark do not inherit their generalization guarantees.
- [Raykar et al. (2010)](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume11/raykar10a/raykar10a.pdf), §1.1 p.1299, §2.4 pp.1301–1302 and §7 p.1319: multiple imperfect labels do not identify a correct reference without additional assumptions. Separate weather/SARAH predictions preserve ambiguity; they do not remove shared errors or become ground truth.

Potential Aktina contribution: an independently audited composition of separate irradiance-reference heads, baseline-preserving paired corrections, explicit prediction clocks and per-reference realized correction ledgers. Prior-art searching here does not establish world-first novelty. Any contribution claim must name the implemented mechanism and measured workload.

## Exact algebra and its limits

For reference event Y, baseline call b, and unit error loss, the gain from flipping b is

`r = 1[b != Y] - 1[1-b != Y] = (1-2b)(2Y-1)`.

Given estimated event probability p_j for reference j, estimated gain is `(1-2b)(2p_j-1)`. Requiring both gains positive is ordinary agreement gating: add when both probabilities exceed .5; remove when both fall below .5. Renaming this expression “regret” does not invent a model, and a positive estimated gain cannot guarantee a realized gain. A cost-sensitive variant would require declared costs, not invented economic values.

For a fixed scored cohort with baseline TP=t, FP=f, unchanged reference labels and defined denominators, recall does not decrease iff ΔTP>=0. Precision does not decrease iff `f*ΔTP-t*ΔFP>=0`. Additions contribute `(Y,1-Y)` and removals `(-Y,-(1-Y))`; replacing Y with p gives estimated contributions only. Precision constraints enforced independently on batches need not preserve aggregate precision when baseline rates differ.

A paired exchange removes one baseline-positive hour r and adds one baseline-negative hour a. It preserves the positive-call count and has realized `ΔTP_j=Y_j(a)-Y_j(r)` and `ΔFP_j=-ΔTP_j`. Therefore, on a complete fixed cohort, precision, recall and F1 move in the same direction as total ΔTP, whenever defined. The estimated pair gain is `g_j=p_j(a)-p_j(r)`. Requiring positive gains for both references is stronger than accepting their average; no independence assumption is needed for these expectation identities. Correct estimates and future benefit are still unproven.

This is a useful restricted form of batch count budgets: it avoids an unconstrained optimizer and directly removes the mechanism by which extra positive calls alone improved recall in 007. It cannot correct a baseline error in the total number of event hours.

## Falsifier and required counterexamples

The product hypothesis fails on this workload if the locked primary policy regresses any of the six full-weather/satellite-common test metrics versus ECMWF, or makes no strict gain. The proposed reference-preserving mechanism has no demonstrated advantage over averaging if its locked result does not improve minimum reference precision while retaining each reference's recall relative to the matched mean-gain ablation. Neither outcome constitutes independent confirmation: the historical test has already informed this research sequence.

Before scoring, test: all observation endpoints precede issue and shared observed-feature columns remain equal within each day; target-varying geometry/forecast columns do not import observations after issue; complete24-hour actions are unchanged when scoring masks change; conflicting gains (+.4,-.2) are rejected by minimum but can pass average; exact margin equality is rejected; no eligible pair retains baseline; one exchange preserves full positive-call count; same observed labels give ΔFP=-ΔTP; cross-mask exchange breaks that identity on the restricted subset; estimated positive gains can produce negative realized gains. Also retain a batch-aggregation counterexample: baseline(t,f)=(90,10) and(1,9), then add one true/four false positives only to the second batch. Its precision improves from.10 to2/15 while aggregate precision falls from91/110 to92/115.

No optimizer restart, test-driven margin change, data fetch, scheduler change or claim of physical curtailment, costs, safety or guaranteed improvement belongs to this experiment. Its value must come from an executed comparison, not the algebra or the name.
