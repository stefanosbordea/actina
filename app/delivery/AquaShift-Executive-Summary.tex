\begin{minipage}[t]{.48\linewidth}
{\large\bfseries What we have}\par
An operations workspace and a local experiment. A machine-learning radiation reference identifies sunny hours. Pick a day and tank size; inspect production, storage and forecast errors. Compare plans with 62 reported grid windows and record a review.

The sunny-hour flag is a proxy. Actual curtailed electricity absorbed remains unmeasured.
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{.47\linewidth}
{\large\bfseries What we need}\par
A water operator's demand, tank and plant limits, plus grid curtailment signals. Test whether extra daytime production can safely absorb otherwise lost electricity and reduce operating costs while maintaining water supply.

Grid serves water operators. Sites, a separate garden and leak demo, offers a smaller pilot route. Pricing still needs customer evidence.
\end{minipage}
