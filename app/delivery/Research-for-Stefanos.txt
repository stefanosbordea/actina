FOR STEFANOS — FOUR USEFUL READS

Forecast inputs: use archived issued weather runs, keeping the initialization and availability time. A weather reconstruction cannot show what was known at 18:00.
Open-Meteo: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/previous-runs-api

Forecast checks: compare with safe persistence and a clear-sky control; if the model supplies quantiles, check their calibration and proper scores. Do not manufacture a confidence band.
Gneiting, Lerch & Schulz (2023): https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2022.12.054

Plant limits: pump efficiency, pressure, minimum operation, flushing and water quality affect how far production can shift. The current 3.4 kWh/m³ scenario does not model these.
Hu & Konstantinou (2026 preprint): https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07295
Author parameter workbook: https://github.com/rxhu89/paper_desali_flex (not rerun)

Pilot value: compare equal water service, power capacity, energy capacity and cost against current operation and a competent simple schedule. Savings depend on the actual tariff and spare capacity.
Rao et al. (2024): https://doi.org/10.1038/s44221-024-00316-4
Author data/code: https://github.com/we3lab/valuing-flexibility-from-water

Cyprus context: the EAC July/August reports now supply 62 dated curtailment windows in the package and app. These are grid reports, not plant measurements. Main SCADA estimates, Group 1 windows and separate Ripple/IoT disconnections must stay distinct.
EAC: https://www.eac.com.cy/EL/RegulatedActivities/Distribution/DistributionSystemOperation/Pages/res-e_curtailments.aspx

Before a pilot: confirm the asset's grid permission and operating limits. EAC's emergency fourth stage stops all PV production; sunshine alone cannot establish usable electricity. DuPont's current FilmTec manual advises smooth pressure changes and fewer starts; flushing and water acceptance affect the usable output. Neither document supplies approved Paphos settings.
EAC procedure: https://www.eac.com.cy/EN/RegulatedActivities/Distribution/DistributionSystemOperation/Documents/Photovoltaic_Disconnection_Process_EN.pdf
DuPont, Rev. 20 (August 2026): https://www.dupont.com/content/dam/water/amer/us/en/water/public/documents/en/RO-NF-FilmTec-Manual-45-D01504-en.pdf
